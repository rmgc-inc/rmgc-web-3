Who you're hiring

A contractor who shows up, and finishes

RMGC Construction Inc is a North York–based construction company led by Roger, working across Toronto and the surrounding GTA. We take on residential renovations and commercial build-outs — and the responsibility that comes with them.

That means arriving when we say we will, protecting your floors and furniture before the first tool comes out, flagging problems we find behind the walls instead of covering them up, and leaving the space cleaner than we found it. Most of our work comes from referrals and repeat clients — the only endorsement in this trade that really counts.

Common questions

Everything you need to know before we begin

What types of projects do you take on?

We handle home renovations, kitchens and bathrooms, basement finishing, condo renovations, and commercial build-outs including office and retail fit-outs.

Where do you provide service?

Our team serves North York, Toronto, Vaughan, Richmond Hill, Markham, Mississauga, and communities throughout the surrounding GTA.

How does the estimate process work?

Call us to talk through your project. We’ll arrange a site visit, understand the scope, and provide a clear written estimate. Estimates are always free.

Are you insured?

Yes. RMGC Construction Inc. carries $5 million in general liability insurance and is WSIB insured and in good standing. Certificates are available on request before work begins.
